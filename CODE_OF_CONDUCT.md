# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus technical feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be clear about assumptions, limitations, and tradeoffs.
- Keep discussions welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Handle disagreement through evidence, examples, and practical next steps.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or private project data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Any relevant links, screenshots, or logs.
- Whether immediate action is needed.

Maintainers should handle reports with discretion and avoid sharing private details beyond what is needed to resolve the issue.
